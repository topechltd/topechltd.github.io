GXE224C

CAN · RS485 · RS232 · 5cm × 5cm Expansion Module

The GXE224C expansion module provides 1-channel CAN, 1-channel RS485, and 2-channel RS232 interfaces in a compact 5cm × 5cm form factor. It supports both screw terminal blocks and 2.54mm pin header connectors for flexible wiring. Engineering files and schematics are provided for integration.
