GXR0115

Programmable Resistor · 1Ω–15MΩ · 0.01% Accuracy

The GXR0115 is an ultra-high-precision programmable resistor with a resistance range from 1Ω to 15MΩ and a minimum step of 2mΩ. In the commonly used 100Ω–1MΩ range, accuracy reaches 0.01%. It employs magnetic latching relays that retain resistance values even after power loss. Controlled via shell command line, it is ideal for sensor calibration, resistance simulation, and precision signal source applications.
