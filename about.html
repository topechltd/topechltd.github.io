About TOPECH

TOPECH was founded in 2020 as a high-tech enterprise focused on the measurement and control industry. The company is dedicated to the research, development, and sales of hardware and software products in data acquisition, signal analysis, and industrial control.

We offer multiple series of high-precision data acquisition cards, control signal output modules, data transmission modules, wireless sensor nodes, and function expansion boards. Our products are widely used in vibration testing, noise analysis, strain measurement, industrial monitoring, and laboratory precision measurement.

Located in Tianfu New Area, Chengdu, we have an independent R&D team and support customized development and system integration services for our clients.
