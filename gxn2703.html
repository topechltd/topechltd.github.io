GXN2703 — 3‑Axis Wireless Vibration Sensor Node

3‑Axis Acceleration · Wireless · IP‑X8 Sensor Node

The GXN2703 is a highly integrated 3-axis vibration acquisition node in a compact 30mm × 30mm × 34mm form factor. It features a low-power MCU, high-precision 3-axis vibration sensor, UWB wireless communication module, 200mAh lithium battery, strong magnetic adsorption, and M4 threaded mounting. Ideal for wireless remote monitoring of rotating machinery, mobile equipment, and hard-to-wire field installations.
