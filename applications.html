Application Scenarios

From laboratory to industrial sites — covering diverse measurement & control needs

Vibration Testing & Analysis

Mechanical equipment vibration monitoring, modal analysis, and fault diagnosis. Supports AC/DC/IEPE multiple sensor types for comprehensive vibration data acquisition.

Recommended: GXA5104 / GXA3801

Strain & Pressure Testing

Structural strain measurement, pressure vessel testing, and material mechanics experiments with high-precision synchronous multi-channel data acquisition.

Recommended: GXA3810 / GXA3801

Noise & Acoustic Testing

Environmental noise monitoring, acoustic analysis, and audio signal acquisition with high-frequency sampling support for precise sound measurement.

Recommended: GXA5104

Industrial Production Line Monitoring

Production line equipment status monitoring, tightening quality control, and process parameter acquisition with CAN/RS485 communication support.

Recommended: GXE1020 / GXE1021 / GXE224C

Laboratory Precision Measurement
